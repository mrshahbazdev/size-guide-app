# Privacy Policy

## Size Guide + Fit Finder

Last updated: July 2026

This app collects the minimum data required to provide size charts, fit finder recommendations and analytics to merchants.

### Data we collect
- **Shop domain**: to identify the store and render the correct size charts.
- **Product identifiers**: product handle, type, tags and collections to match size charts.
- **Customer ID (when logged in)**: to save measurement profiles across sessions.
- **Measurements**: chest, waist, hips and other measurements entered by the shopper.
- **Event data**: size guide opens, fit finder submissions and recommended sizes for analytics.

### How we use data
- Match products to the right size charts.
- Provide fit finder results and measurement-based recommendations.
- Show merchants anonymized analytics about feature usage.

### Data storage
Data is stored in the merchant's own Shopify app database or the hosting database configured by the merchant. We do not sell or share data with third parties.

### Data retention
Analytics and measurement profiles are retained as long as the merchant keeps the app installed. Merchants can delete data from the app dashboard.

### Contact
For privacy questions, contact the app developer through the Shopify Partner Dashboard.
